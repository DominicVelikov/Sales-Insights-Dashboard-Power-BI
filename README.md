# Power BI Sales Insights Dashboard

## Dashboard Preview

![Sales Insights Dashboard](./sales-insights-dashboard)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and profitability for a hypothetical mid-size U.S. retailer selling Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology products across four regions.

The objective was to create a self-service Power BI dashboard that gives leadership a clear view of sales performance, profitability, seasonality, regional results, product performance, discounting, and customer segments.

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Sales:** $2.06M
- **Total Profit:** $449.75K
- **Profit Margin:** 21.9%
- **Order-Line Records:** 7,649
- **Year-over-Year Growth:** 42.3%
- **Date Range:** January 2023 – December 2025

## Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer:

1. How are sales, profit, margin, order volume, and year-over-year growth performing?
2. Which months consistently generate the strongest demand?
3. Which categories and sub-categories generate the most sales and profit?
4. Which regions and states are leading or trailing?
5. Where might discounting be reducing profitability?
6. How do Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customers compare?

## Key Insights

- November and December consistently produce the strongest sales peaks.
- Furniture leads with approximately $1.00M in sales and $0.24M in profit.
- The Central region leads in sales, while the East region trails.
- Consumer customers provide the most sales volume.
- Corporate customers have the highest profit margin.
- Home Office customers have the strongest year-over-year growth.
- Technology products generate meaningful profit at comparatively lower discount levels.

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive date, region, state, category, sub-category, and segment slicers
- Executive KPI cards
- Region-to-state drill-down hierarchy
- Category-to-sub-category drill-down hierarchy
- Monthly sales and seasonality analysis
- Sales and profit comparisons
- Profit-versus-discount analysis
- Dynamic cross-filtering across visuals

## Development Process

1. Imported and validated the retail sales dataset in Power BI.
2. Reviewed column types and confirmed data quality.
3. Created a dedicated Date table and related it to the order data.
4. Developed reusable DAX measures for sales, profit, margin, orders, and YoY growth.
5. Created geographic and product hierarchies for drill-down analysis.
6. Designed interactive KPI cards, bar charts, a trend chart, and a scatter plot.
7. Tested slicers, cross-filtering, calculations, and dashboard readability.
8. Applied a consistent navy and slate visual theme.

## Recommendations

- Prepare inventory, staffing, and fulfillment capacity before November.
- Establish sub-category-specific discount limits to protect margins.
- Investigate pricing, assortment, and customer mix in the East region.
- Preserve Consumer scale while expanding Corporate and Home Office opportunities.
